# Anti-slop audit 001

Date: 2026-09-22
Scope: AOS server-rendered frontend after the Dispatch Manifest revamp
Mode: post-build audit only. No findings were modified.

## Findings

### 1. HIGH: Long mobile text still clips at 390px

Rules: R-03, C-4

Evidence: The final Impeccable verdict found long task titles, guardrail text, and run summaries cut at the right edge in `mobile.png` and `task-mobile.png`. Progress checkpoints and checksums now fit, but these prose regions do not consistently wrap.

Why it matters: A mid-range Android phone is a primary use case. Hidden task or guardrail text can remove operational context and safety constraints.

Suggested fix: Remove the remaining width constraint or clipping source in the affected task and card descendants, then verify the exact long-content fixtures at 390px and 200 percent text zoom.

Status: Accepted by the user for this ship.

### 2. HIGH: Compact controls fall below the 44px touch target

Rule: R-03

Evidence: `templates/base.html` sets ordinary buttons to `min-height: 44px`, then overrides `.small` buttons and links to `min-height: 36px`. These compact controls are used for Queue health, Done, Retry, Open, and other frequent mobile actions.

Why it matters: The primary users often operate AOS on Android. A 36px target is harder to hit reliably with a thumb.

Suggested fix: Keep the compact visual treatment while restoring a 44px hit area on touch layouts, either globally or inside the mobile media query.

### 3. HIGH: Open notice dialogs do not provide a verified Escape path

Rules: R-26, R-32

Evidence: `templates/queue.html` and `templates/task.html` render `<dialog open>` directly. They provide an OK button, but no script calls `showModal()` and no verified keyboard behavior ensures Escape closes the non-modal dialog.

Why it matters: Keyboard users need a predictable way to dismiss an interruption. A rendered-open dialog also lacks the focus management of a modal dialog opened through the dialog API.

Suggested fix: Use a small progressive-enhancement script to call `showModal()`, focus the dismiss action, and preserve native Escape handling, while keeping the existing server-rendered fallback.

### 4. HIGH: Visible UI copy contains widespread em dash characters

Rule: R-02

Evidence: The templates contain 73 matches. Examples include page titles, empty-value placeholders, task guidance, gate language, and status copy in `agent.html`, `health.html`, `new.html`, `_taskfields.html`, `queue.html`, `task.html`, and `file.html`.

Why it matters: This is a hard-gate style rule selected for the post-build audit. The character appears often enough to become a repeated writing tell rather than an isolated editorial choice.

Suggested fix: Replace prose uses with periods, commas, colons, or parentheses. Replace empty-value placeholders with a deliberate value such as `Not assigned` or `None recorded`, rather than punctuation alone.

### 5. MEDIUM: The clipped manifest corner is applied more broadly than the documented rule

Rules: R-11, R-31

Evidence: `DESIGN.md` says clipped upper-right corners belong to manifest-like containers and should not appear on every container. `templates/base.html` applies the treatment to both `.panel` and every `.card`, including the full agent directory.

Why it matters: Repeating the signature device everywhere weakens its meaning and makes content types less distinct.

Suggested fix: Restrict the clipped corner and registration mark to manifest, review, and governance-state containers. Leave ordinary directory and supporting cards on the simple 4px container treatment.

## Audit result

The revamp has a specific visual identity, real data, working routes, explicit governance states, verified contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and 52 passing tests. It does not pass the anti-slop delivery gate because findings 1 through 4 are HIGH priority.

Choose finding numbers before any post-ship fixes are made.
